Policies & Regulations

Official BEZENT Corporate Attendance Guidelines.

1. Standard Roster & Working Hours

All general employees are scheduled on the General Shift which begins at 09:00 AM and ends at 06:00 PM. A standard workday accounts for 8 active working hours excluding the 1-hour lunch break interval.

Daily check-in and check-out logs are mapped through biometric verification, standard biometric scanner punch, or authorized web punch. Missing punches must be regularized within 48 hours.

2. Late Arrivals & Monthly Credits

The shift grace start time is 09:30 AM. Arrivals registered between 09:30 AM and 09:35 AM are allowed under minor tolerance rules.

Arrivals registered after 09:35 AM deduct from the starting balance of 40 late arrival credits. 1 credit is deducted per minute of delay. A delay of 34 minutes (e.g. check-in at 09:34 AM) deducts 4 credits. Delay exceeding 5 minutes caps at 5 credits and registers as a Half Day status. If credits are exhausted, further delays register as LOP (Loss of Pay).

3. Short Permission Rules

Employees are eligible to request up to 2 short permission leaves per month. Each permission allows absence from regular shift duties for a maximum duration of 2 hours.

Approved permissions must be scheduled in advance and do not lead to worked hour deductions, provided the shift total is at least 6 active hours.

4. Half Day Classification

Daily working hours are evaluated according to the following metric guidelines:

  • Present: Active work duration is at least 8.0 hours.
  • Half Day: Active work duration is between 4.5 hours and 8.0 hours, or arrival is delayed by more than 5 minutes.
  • Absent: Active work duration is less than 4.5 hours, or no punches are registered.
